Ingest beta: how to install it, and what to expect

INSTALLING

macOS will refuse to open this beta the first time, because it isn't notarised yet. You only have to get past that once for each build.

1. Drag Ingest to Applications.
2. Open Ingest from Applications. macOS says it can't be opened. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security, and scroll down to Security.
4. Beside the message about Ingest, click Open Anyway, then enter your login password. The button stays there for about an hour after you tried to open the app.
5. Click Open.

On macOS 14 you can skip all that: Control-click the app and choose Open.

WHAT TO EXPECT

- There's no window and no Dock icon. Ingest lives in the menu bar, as a card icon. Click it for the widget. If the menu bar is full and the icon is hidden, open Ingest from Applications again.
- The first time it reads a card or a drive, macOS asks whether it may read removable volumes. Say yes. Each new beta build asks again.
- Your working drive can be exFAT, APFS or HFS+. FAT32 works, but refuses files of 4 GB or more. macOS 14 or later.
- On your drive it leaves .ingest, a hidden folder with your folder setup and a record of what was copied, and ascmhl beside each card's footage, the checksum manifest. Your cards are never written to.

BEFORE YOU FORMAT ANYTHING

Don't format a card on this beta's word alone without keeping another copy, until you're told otherwise. Clips aren't played: it proves the bytes match, and a clip that was already broken will match perfectly.

Take a card or the drive out and the proof ends. Put the card back in and it's read in full again before it can say Safe.

If something goes wrong, Report a Bug in the ··· menu copies a report for you. Send it to the person who gave you the app.
